❓ NEXUS Strategy Guide & Rules

⚡ 3×3 Standard Grid Target: 3 in a Row
The timeless classic duel. Align 3 of your marks horizontally, vertically, or along either diagonal before your rival does.
💠 4×4 Expanded Grid Target: 4 in a Row
Increased spatial complexity with 16 total tiles. Align 4 marks across rows, columns, or corner-to-corner diagonals.
🌌 5×5 Arena Grid Target: 4 in a Row
High-stakes warfare across 25 tiles. Connect 4 consecutive marks anywhere on the grid in any orthogonal or diagonal vector to secure victory.
💡 Tactical Tip: Secure the central quadrants to multiply your potential victory vectors.
🔄 Vanishing Marks Dynamic Zero Stalemate
Stalemates and cat's games are mathematically prevented! Each player can only maintain a strict maximum number of marks on the board at once:

• 3×3 Grid: Max 3 marks per player.
• 4×4 & 5×5 Grids: Max 4 marks per player.
⏳ The Disappearing Cue Visual Warning
When you have reached your mark cap, your oldest mark flashes with a red warning badge (⏳ Next). As soon as you place your next mark, that oldest tile smoothly dissolves into thin air!
💡 Strategy: Plan 2 steps ahead. Sometimes letting an old mark vanish opens up a winning surprise strike!
⏱️ 8-Second Turn Constraint High Pressure
No over-thinking or stalling allowed. You have exactly 8.0 seconds to assess the board, formulate a counter-strategy, and place your mark.
⚠️ Audio & Visual Countdown Critical Zone
The neon timer bar shrinks above the status HUD. When remaining time dips under 2.5 seconds, the bar shifts to urgent red with rhythmic warning ticks.
⚡ Timeout Penalty Forced Play
If your turn timer hits zero, the arena mainframe instantly claims a random unoccupied cell on your behalf to maintain momentum.
🤖 Impossible Protocol Minimax Engine
Powered by recursive Minimax decision analysis. The bot evaluates every possible branch of the game tree. It will NEVER make a blunder and cannot be defeated under classic rules.
🛡️ Medium Protocol Tactical Heuristic
A balanced sparring partner. It will actively seize winning shots and reliably block your immediate threats, but occasionally leaves subtle peripheral openings.
🌱 Easy Protocol Casual Sparring
Great for relaxed games and learning new modes. Plays with a large degree of randomness and relaxed defensive awareness.
